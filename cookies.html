Cookie Policy

This page explains the cookies and similar technologies that may be used on www.equilibriorecords.com.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device. They can remember preferences, measure visits or enable third-party players.

2. Cookies we may use

Strictly necessary

Needed for the site to work (for example language preference on this device). These do not require extra consent.

Analytics (optional)

This website is prepared to use Google Analytics. Those cookies help us understand how the public catalog is used. They should only run if you accept analytics cookies.

Third-party media

If you play SoundCloud, YouTube or Beatport embeds, those platforms may set their own cookies according to their policies.

3. Equilibrio AI

The assistant does not need advertising cookies. A question you send is a request to our server, not a marketing cookie.

4. Cookie settings

Until a full consent banner is installed, you can control cookies from your browser:

  • Block or delete cookies in the browser settings
  • Use the browser’s private/incognito mode
  • Disable analytics add-ons if you installed any

A dedicated accept/reject panel can be added later without changing the look of the homepage.
